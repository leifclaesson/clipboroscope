What’s on the glass

It shows you everything. Then it lets you zoom in.

Most clipboard viewers show you one flavour of what you copied. Clipboroscope shows you all of them at once, renders the ones it understands, and refreshes the instant the clipboard changes.

▤

Every format at once

Text, Unicode, images, HTML, rich text, file drops, audio, and raw bytes — the full list on the clipboard right now.

⊕

Per-format zoom

Snaps to round levels, remembered separately for text-like formats and images. Audio gets its own time-span zoom.

↻

Live refresh

Watches the clipboard and re-reads the moment it changes. What you see is always what’s on the glass.

◉

Plumbing, tagged

The OLE bookkeeping formats browsers and Office leave behind are marked as plumbing, so they never hijack the view.

◆

DPI-aware

Sharp on every monitor and scale factor. Built for Windows 11, dressed like it escaped from 1998.

⎙

Replaces clipbrd.exe

The clipboard viewer Windows quietly retired, brought back and taken seriously — for once.

Provenance Unverifiable

It has the unmistakable air of weird shareware from 1998. It looks like it was recovered from a forgotten FTP server around 2002. It runs flawlessly on Windows 11.

It is, in fact, a brand-new Rust program. None of these facts are reconcilable. That is the joke, and it is load-bearing.

Name chosen during a moment of severe judgment impairment.
